# Interface conventions

- Do not use visible step counters or numbered process labels such as "Step 1 of 3", "01 / Details", or numbered circles in progress indicators.
- Organize flows with clear section names, concise instructions, and state-based feedback. When order matters, communicate it through layout and controls instead of numbering the stages.
- Counts that describe real data, limits, pagination, route stops, or check-ins are allowed. They must not be presented as decorative workflow progress.
